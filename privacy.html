Privacy Policy

DocScan - PDF Editor and Scan

HomePrivacyTermsHelp

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains how DocScan - PDF Editor and Scan ("DocScan", "the app", "we") handles your information. DocScan is built to keep your documents private: they are stored on your device, and nothing is uploaded unless you choose to back up.

1. Your documents

  • Scans, PDFs, images and the text recognised in them are stored only on your device.
  • Text recognition (OCR) and document insights run on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Your pages are not sent to us or any server.
  • We never see, read, sell or use the contents of your documents.

2. Optional cloud backup

If you turn on backup, your PDFs are uploaded directly from your device to the storage account you connect — Google Drive or iCloud Drive. Files go to a DocScan folder in your own account. We do not receive or store copies. Each provider's own privacy policy applies to files stored with them. You can turn backup off at any time in Settings → Cloud backup.

DocScan requests only the narrowest access needed: Google Drive access is limited to files the app creates (drive.file); it cannot see your other Drive files.

3. Account and sign-in (optional)

An account is not required — you can use every document feature as a guest. If you sign in with Apple, Google or email, we receive your name, email address and an account ID (Apple may provide a private relay email). This is used only to identify your account. Accounts are managed by our authentication provider, Supabase. Sign-in tokens are stored securely in the iOS Keychain.

4. Advertising

DocScan is free and shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking), approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) we ask for your consent first, and iOS asks your permission before any tracking. You can change this in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Permissions

  • Camera — to scan documents.
  • Photos — to import images you choose, and to save pages when you ask.
  • Contacts — only when you choose to save a scanned business card; the system "New Contact" screen is used and we don't read your contacts.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't build profiles from your documents.
  • We don't use analytics that read your files.
  • There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

7. Data retention and deletion

Documents stay on your device until you delete them (Files → Trash → Empty Trash) or delete the app. You can delete your account at any time in the app: profile photo → Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your sign-in and account data from our servers and disconnects Google. Files in your own cloud storage are not touched — you can delete them there.

8. Children

DocScan is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data and to withdraw consent. Contact us using the email below and we'll respond within 30 days.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
