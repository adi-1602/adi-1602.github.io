Privacy Policy

DocScanner: PDF Editor and Scanner

HomePrivacyTermsHelp

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains how DocScanner: PDF Editor and Scanner ("DocScanner", "the app", "we") handles your information. DocScanner is built to keep your documents private: they are stored on your device, and nothing is uploaded unless you choose to back up.

1. Your documents

  • Scans, PDFs, images and the text recognised in them are stored only on your device.
  • Text recognition (OCR) runs on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Your pages are not sent to us or any server for OCR.
  • We never see, read, sell or use the contents of your documents.

2. Optional cloud backup

If you turn on backup, your PDFs are uploaded directly from your device to the storage account you connect — Google Drive, iCloud Drive, Microsoft OneDrive or Dropbox. Files go to a DocScanner folder in your own account. We do not receive or store copies. Each provider's own privacy policy applies to files stored with them. You can disconnect a provider at any time in Settings → Cloud backup.

DocScanner requests only the narrowest access needed: Google Drive access is limited to files the app creates (drive.file), and Dropbox / OneDrive access is limited to the app's own folder.

3. Account and sign-in (optional)

You can use DocScanner as a guest. If you sign in with Apple, Google or email, we receive your name and email address (Apple may provide a private relay email). This is used only to identify your account and, if you enable backup, to connect your cloud storage. Email accounts are managed by our authentication provider, Supabase. Sign-in tokens are stored securely in the iOS Keychain.

4. Advertising

Free users see banner ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier), approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) we ask for your consent first, and iOS asks your permission before any tracking. You can change these choices in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Premium removes ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Purchases

DocScanner Premium is purchased through Apple's App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. We only receive confirmation of whether your subscription is active.

6. Permissions

  • Camera — to scan documents.
  • Photos — to import images you choose, and to save pages or original photos when you ask.
  • Contacts — only when you choose to save a scanned business card; the system "New Contact" screen is used and we don't read your contacts.

7. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't build profiles from your documents.
  • We don't use analytics that read your files.

8. Data retention and deletion

Documents stay on your device until you delete them (Files → Trash → Empty Trash) or delete the app. You can delete your account at any time in the app: profile photo → Settings → Delete account. This removes your sign-in and any account data on our servers. Files in your own cloud storage are not touched — you can delete them there.

9. Children

DocScanner is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data and to withdraw consent. Contact us using the email below and we'll respond within 30 days.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
