How do I scan a document?

Tap the purple Scan button. Hold steady over the page — DocScanner finds the edges and captures automatically. Adjust the borders if needed, tap Keep, then Done ✓ → Save PDF.

What do Book, ID card and Business card modes do?

Book splits a two-page spread into two pages. ID card puts the front and back on one page. Business card offers to save the details to Contacts. Whiteboard reduces glare and shadows.

My page came out tilted

On the Review screen tap Crop and drag the four corners to the page edges. Tap Auto-detect to try again.

How do I sign a PDF?

Open the PDF → Edit → Sign. Draw your signature once; it's saved for next time. Drag to move it and use the corner handle to resize.

How do I make a scan searchable / copy its text?

Open the document → Text. Recognition runs on your iPhone. Then Copy, Export .txt or Create searchable PDF. Try the 📖 Reader view for comfortable reading.

How do I back up my files?

Profile photo → Settings → Cloud backup → choose Google Drive, iCloud Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox → Back up now. Backup is always optional.

How do I cancel Premium?

iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → DocScanner → Cancel. To restore on a new device: Settings in the app → Subscriptions → Restore Purchases.

How do I delete my account?

Profile photo → Settings → Delete account. Documents on your device and in your own cloud aren't affected.
