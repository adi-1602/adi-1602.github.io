Do I need an account?

No. Tap Continue as guest on the sign-in screen and every scanning and editing feature works. Signing in is only needed if you want an account; backup to your own Google Drive or iCloud Drive is optional.

How do I scan a document?

Tap the purple Scan button. Hold steady over the page — DocScan finds the edges and captures automatically. Adjust the borders if needed and tap Done, then Save PDF.

What do Book, ID card and Business card modes do?

Book splits a two-page spread into two pages. ID card puts the front and back on one page. Business card offers to save the details to Contacts. Whiteboard reduces glare and shadows.

My page came out tilted

On the Review screen tap Crop and drag the four corners or edges to the page. Tap Auto-detect to try again.

How do I sign a PDF?

Open the PDF → Edit → Sign. Draw your signature once; it's saved for next time. Drag to move it and use the corner handle to resize.

How do I search inside a PDF?

Open the PDF and tap the search icon. Matches are highlighted; use ↑/↓ to move between them or open the results list. If a scan has no text yet, tap Recognise text first.

How do I make a scan searchable / copy its text?

Open the document → Text. Recognition runs on your iPhone. Then Copy, Export .txt or Create searchable PDF.

How do I back up my files?

Profile photo → Settings → Cloud backup → choose Google Drive or iCloud Drive → Back up now. Files appear in a DocScan folder (Google Drive: My Drive → DocScan; iCloud: Files app → iCloud Drive → DocScan). Backup is always optional.

Is DocScan really free?

Yes. Every feature is free with no limits, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Small banner ads keep it free.

How do I delete my account?

Profile photo → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account from our servers. Documents on your device and in your own cloud aren't affected.
